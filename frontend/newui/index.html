What delivery models does JNAB offer?

We provide three primary models: Full-Cycle Product Builds (we own end-to-end design, engineering, and release), Embedded Senior Talent (vetted engineers integrated directly into your team), and Autonomous Tech Pods (complete cross-functional squads dedicated to your product roadmap).

Where are your engineers based and how do you handle timezones?

Our engineering headquarters is located in Accra, Ghana, operating on GMT (West Africa Time). This provides seamless same-day working hour overlap with UK, European, and US East Coast teams.

How does JNAB vet its software engineers?

We run a rigorous 4-stage evaluation process assessing computer science foundations, system architecture, code craftsmanship, and proactive communication. Only the top 1% join our client engineering network.

Who owns the intellectual property (IP)?

You own 100% of all intellectual property, source code, design files, and documentation from day one. All work is conducted under strict confidentiality and IP assignment agreements.

How quickly can a project or team start?

Following our initial discovery session and blueprint alignment, dedicated senior engineers or autonomous pods can be active in your codebase within 1 to 2 weeks.

What is JNAB Academy?

JNAB Academy is our technical training powerhouse cultivating high-potential African talent with intensive training in full-stack software engineering, mobile development, and UI/UX design.

Can our company hire JNAB Academy graduates?

Yes. We partner with global startups and scale-ups seeking pre-vetted, mentored junior-to-mid software engineers and product designers.
